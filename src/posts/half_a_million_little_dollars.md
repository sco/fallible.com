If you haven't seen <a href="http://www.thecrimson.com/article.aspx?ref=512948" title="this story">this story</a>, you should.

Honestly, what are people thinking when they sit down to claim a hefty $500,000 advance from Little, Brown? Do writers really imagine that the technology doesn't exist to discover whether or not they've plagiarized? How does a chick get herself into Harvard without knowing she shouldn't copy chick lit from the chick in front of her?

James Frey messed up big-time, claiming fact where there was merely story. But this gal? In my opinion, this is even a more agregious literary offense.

But, hey. Who am I to judge? I don't even like it if I think I might have copied myself.
