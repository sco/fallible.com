"Men, it has been well said, think in herds; it will be seen that they go mad in herds, while they only recover their senses slowly, and one by one." Charles Mackey

A New York Times article is reporting that more than two thirds of Americans admit to being depressed, many unable to sleep and multitudes unable to focus on their work.

"Homeland Security" may restore our collective mental health, or maybe it will only defend the ragged borders of our minds against fresh, virulent attacks from without. What about the parts deep in the interior, where anguished hostages are already held without ransom?

Heartland Security is accomplished slowly, personally, one by one. By the One who knows each heart, and knows what it will take to heal each heart.

Homeland Security for all of us. Heartland Security for each of us.
