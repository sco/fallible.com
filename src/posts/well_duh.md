---
title: "Well, Duh!"
date: "2005-06-09T03:06:02"
---

We are getting absolutely pounded here with torrential rains and 70 to 90-mile-per-hour straight-line winds. The weather guy I was watching until my roof nearly flew into my back yard hadn't even MENTIONED our county as being in the path of the storms. But then I looked out the window and saw an acre's worth of trees in a suspiciously horizontal position.

Right now I'm thinking about my daughter's boyfriend, who's getting a Master's in meteorology. I hope for as long as I know him I never hear him say what I just heard a Kansas City weatherman say.

"After the rest of these storms pass through, the threat of severe weather will be significantly diminished."
