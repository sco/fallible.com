My favorite t-shirt used to be the one that says, "What If the Hokey-Pokey Really Is What It's All About?"

I can't even use it for a nightshirt now, it seems so shallow.

My second favorite t-shirt says, "If A Man Speaks In the Forest, And There Is No Woman There to Hear Him, Is He Still Wrong?"

This one will feel OK to wear again in public someday, but who can say when?

My final favorite t-shirt says, "Chocolate--It's Not Just for Breakfast Anymore."

To everything, turn, turn, turn, there is a season. And a time for every t-shirt under heaven.
