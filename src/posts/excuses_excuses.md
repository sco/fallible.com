Last year at this time, I was getting ready to head to the American Christian Writers Conference in Dallas. This year at this time, I'm doing the exact same thing.

The difference is that while last year I felt pretty good about having typed "The End" at the bottom of a 90.000 word manuscript, this year I realize it wasn't really ready to submit. So, several drafts later, I think I've about got it wrapped up.

I'll head out next Wednesday and be gone through Sunday. Until then, I'm tying up loose plot ends, formatting, printing out revamped proposals and sample chapters, and getting mentally ready to meet with editors and agents.

And to see some of my favorite friends in the world, my fellow novelists!

If you'll overlook my lack of blogging for the next couple weeks, I promise to sign a book for you someday.  :)

How's that for dreaming big?
