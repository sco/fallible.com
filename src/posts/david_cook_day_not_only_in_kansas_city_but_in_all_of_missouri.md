David Cook, who's now arrived in the top three on American Idol for VERY good reason, was home in Kansas City today to celebrate among his peeps. And celebrate we did!

<a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/katyrose/2478337647/" title="David Cook Kansas City Homecoming" target="cook"><img src="{filedir_1}david-cook-homecoming1_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="David Cook Homecoming" name="image" width="100" height="75" align="left" hspace="6" vspace="6"/></a>Before appearing in a parade in his honor and then singing to a stadium full of fans, David took the stage outdoors in the new Kansas City Power & Light entertainment district downtown. With a canopy overhead to shield the audience from sprinkles and multi-levels for great views, the venue proved a perfect one, especially since it's directly across the street from the acclaimed Sprint Center arena where the top ten Idols will be performing in August.

David opened by seeming genuinely shocked by the turn-out, saying to the crowd, "Why are you all HERE?" I've got to think it's such a different thing to be in the "Idol Bubble" for all these many weeks and then to suddenly be out with the rest of everyone--the regular people who are rooting for you and proud of their hometown boy. He sang <a href="http://www.myfoxkc.com/myfox/MyFox/pages/sidebar_video.jsp?contentId=6505068&version=1&locale=EN-US" title="David Cook: Living on a Prayer" target="cook">Living on a Prayer</a> and <a href="http://www.myfoxkc.com/myfox/MyFox/pages/sidebar_video.jsp?contentId=6505027&version=1&locale=EN-US" title="David Cook: Always Be My Baby">Always Be My Baby</a>.

<a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/katyrose/2479150298/in/set-72157604971792725/" title="David Cook Homecoming Security" target="cook"><img src="{filedir_1}david-cook-security_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="image" name="image" width="100" height="75" align="right" vspace="6" hspace="6" /></a>Speaking of pride, I could not be prouder of my husband Doug, who practically got arrested (okay, that might be a small exaggeration, but not much of one at all considering how excited I am!) by positioning himself and his camera within one foot of the limousine door through which David Cook eventually entered to leave the area.

<a href="http://www.flickr.com/photos/katyrose/2478337243/in/set-72157604971792725/" title="Katy Mckenna w-Kevin and Rocky at David Cook" target="cook"><img src="{filedir_1}katy-mckenna-kevin-rocky_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="image" name="image" width="100" height="75" align="left" vspace="6" hspace="6"  /></a>My son Kevin, our friend Rocky and I stood across the street and watched Doug's boldness increase moment by moment. He was not deterred by officers of the law (who tried to push him out of the way!), by bodyguards (who gave him threatening looks!), or by Kansas City Chiefs cheerleaders (who honestly had on almost no clothing!) from fulfilling his mission of getting THE BEST POSSIBLE close-ups of David that he could.
