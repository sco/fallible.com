title: "(No Title)"
date: "2001-09-17T15:07:12"
---

No matter where their daddy was, they'd always known they could reach him on his cell phone. Hadn't he called four times in a row, Tuesday morning, before he never called again?

"Can we call Daddy on his cell phone?" they asked their mommy, still believing.

"No," she answered, and a new belief slowly dawned on their upturned faces.

"There are no cell phones in heaven."
