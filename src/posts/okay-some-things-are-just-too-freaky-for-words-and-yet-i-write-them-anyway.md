---
title: "Okay, Some Things Are Just Too Freaky For Words, And Yet I Write Them Anyway"
date: "2008-08-02T23:08:00"
---

Doug and I RARELY watch the national news, on any station.

Tonight I turned it on, and was NOT paying attention as I worked on my computer at the same time. It was purely background noise. Doug was working also, in the same room. I'm not sure he even realized I'd turned on the tube.

I just so happened to land on the <a href="http://www.cbsnews.com/sections/i_video/main500251.shtml?id=4317069n" title="CBS NATIONAL NEWS">CBS NATIONAL NEWS</a>, too unmotivated to even flip channels. Out of the corner of my consciousness, I heard the broadcaster say, "When we come back, we'll look at how evangelical Christians in a swing state might be voting in the presidential election."

I thought, "Ho-hum, could I be more bored? OK, I won't turn it off quite yet."

<a href="http://www.cbsnews.com/sections/i_video/main500251.shtml?id=4317069n" title="CBS NATIONAL NEWS"><img src="{filedir_1}swing-voters_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="image" name="image" width="100" height="75" align="left" vspace="4" hspace="4" /></a>After the commercial break, the broadcaster said, "We're going to take a look at evangelical voters in the swing state of Missouri."

My ears perked up. I said to Doug, "Hey, they're going to talk about Missouri." At which point, he turned around from his desk to watch.

<a href="http://www.jacobswellchurch.org/" title="Jacob's Well Church"><img src="{filedir_1}jacobs-well_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="image" name="image" width="100" height="73" align="right" vspace="4" hspace="4" /></a> - The next thing we knew, they flashed a picture of the outside of Scott, Brooke, and Kevin's church, with the name "<a href="http://www.jacobswellchurch.org/">Jacob's Well</a>" underneath. I yelled, "Doug, it's Jacob's Well, on NATIONAL news."

<a href="http://www.timkeel.com/timkeel/2008/08/jw-on-cbs-eveni.html"><img src="{filedir_1}tim-keel_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="image" name="image" width="80" height="69" align="left" vspace="4" hspace="4" /></a>Then they showed the inside of the church, during an actual service, with <a href="http://www.timkeel.com/timkeel/2008/08/jw-on-cbs-eveni.html">Tim Keel</a> preaching (the wonderful man who officiated at Scott and Brooke's wedding).

<a href="{filedir_1}scott-church.jpg"><img src="{filedir_1}scott-church-thumbnail_thumb.jpg" border="0" alt="image" name="image" width="100" height="78" align="right" vspace="4" hspace="4"/></a>THEN the camera turned on the congregation and there was our son SCOTT! About six people on the screen and he was right THERE! One of the weirdest experiences I've EVER had, since WE DON'T EVEN WATCH THE CBS NEWS!!!!

We immediately called Scott, who doesn't own a TV, to tell him what we'd just seen. "Well," he said, ever the philosopher, "maybe you should watch national news more often."

So I'm here to tell you: If any of you get a hankerin' to see one of those adult kids of yours, maybe checking out the national news is just the ticket. It worked for us!
