<p>This may be the best technique yet for promoting a new blog—marry up. The April edition of Writer's Digest Personal Writing magazine includes a blurb about <a href="http://www.fallible.com/">fallible.com</a> (and a mention of marginalcomments.com.)</p>

<p>This follows a trend that started when we got engaged—I didn't own a car of my own, so we shared Katy's. Katy will soon receive her British citizenship, and I will be grafted in with no questions asked. And if Katy ever runs for President, I could turn out to be the first First Husband.</p>

<p>Some guys have all the luck.<br>
