<p><img src="/assets/spire.jpg" width="74" height="101" border="0" align="right" vspace="5" hspace="5">The January '05 issue of Christianity Today offers this story as insight into the state of the church in America:</p>

<p>A man is rescued after twenty years alone on a desert island. His rescuer is astonished to find that the castaway has built several imposing structures.</p>

<p>"Wow!" the rescuer says. "What's that beautiful stone building overlooking the bay?"</p>

<p>"That is my home," the castaway says.</p>

<p>"And what about that building over there, with the spires?"</p>

<p>"That," the castaway says, "is my church."</p>

<p>"But wait!" the rescuer says. "That building over there, with the bell tower. What is that?"</p>

<p>"That is the church I used to belong to."</p>

<p><br>
May your roots go deep in 2005!</p>
